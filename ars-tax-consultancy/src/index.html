FAQ

Common questions

Which accounting software do you use?

We maintain books of accounts in Tally, Zoho Books or Odoo, covering transaction recording, journal and ledger management, period-end closing, bank reconciliation and document maintenance. We can also set up or migrate your data between these platforms.

Can the scope of services be customised?

Yes. The scope can be tailored to the size and volume of your transactions and aligned with management's expectations.

What is not included in the standard monthly scope?

Representation or reply drafting for notices from government authorities, new or amended registrations, certification and attestation work, and Form 15CA/15CB for foreign remittances. These can be taken up as separate assignments, with fees discussed case by case.

How are fees determined?

Fees depend on the agreed scope and the size of your business. Request a proposal and we will share a clear, tailored quote.
