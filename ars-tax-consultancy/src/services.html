GST Compliance

Returns prepared and filed on time, so you stay clear of interest and penalties.

Monthly

GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B

Preparation and monthly filing of both returns. GSTR-1 before the 11th and GSTR-3B before the 20th.

Yearly

LUT Filing

Form GST RFD-11 filed annually, on or before 31st March, to make exports without payment of GST.

Yearly

GST Annual Return & Reconciliation Statement

Annual return where turnover exceeds Rs. 2 crore and reconciliation statement where turnover exceeds Rs. 5 crore, by 31st December.

E-Way Bill & E-Invoice

Prepared from the data you provide, as and when required.
